Who we are

Under Fifty Finds (underfiftyfinds.github.io) publishes gift guides and product recommendations, and shares them on Pinterest at pinterest.com/underfiftyfinds. It is run by Micah Jung in California, USA.

Website visitors

This website does not use accounts, forms, cookies or analytics of its own, and we do not collect personal information from visitors. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. Links to Amazon are affiliate links: if you click one, Amazon may use cookies to credit purchases to us, under Amazon's own privacy notice. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Our Pinterest app ("Under Fifty Finds Publisher")

We use the Pinterest API only to manage our own Pinterest account, @underfiftyfinds: creating pins, listing our own boards, and reading analytics for our own pins. The app is not offered to other Pinterest users.

  • Data we access: our own account's boards, pins and pin analytics, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens Pinterest issues to us.
  • How we use it: to publish our pins and to measure which pins perform best. We do not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest user.
  • Storage and protection: tokens and app credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in our private configuration (GitHub Actions secrets), are never published, and are only used to call the Pinterest API. Analytics numbers for our own pins may be saved in our own records.
  • Sharing: we do not sell or share Pinterest data with anyone.
  • Retention and deletion: we keep data only while the app is in use. Revoking the app's access in Pinterest settings stops all access; we then delete stored tokens.
